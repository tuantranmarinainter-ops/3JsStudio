## CC0 assets used by the example maps

All CC0 1.0. The kit tints some textures, prints its own chevron on the backrooms wallpaper, and decimates and
rescales the models in Blender (`kit/blender/prep_model.py`). Trees, grass and reed tufts are generated
in Blender from CC0 textures; skies are CC0 HDRIs cut into six faces by `kit/tools/sky.py`.
