# Backrooms Level 0: build notes (method by fwjelaa, FWJ STUDIOS)

## Materials (2026-10-24)
- **Wallpaper:** none of ambientCG's six wallpapers has a printed motif (they're plain or peeling white
  plaster). Wallpaper002A (plain, CC0) is the base; the kit tints it mono-yellow #D8C46A and prints its own
  zig-zag chevron (`--motif chevron:128:0.10`, 8 rows per tile, tiles seamlessly). Picked over fine chevrons and
  dots because it reads like the original photo.
- **Carpet:** Carpet012 tinted damp beige #B8A27A, Organic pattern.
- **Ceiling:** OfficeCeiling001, a clean 2x2 tile grid with no lights baked in (so the troffers are real parts),
  6 tiles per texture → StudsPerTile 13.
- **Hum:** generated by `kit/tools/audio.py` (120 Hz + harmonics + ballast buzz), uploaded as Audio.

## QA round 1
`K.qa`: QA FAIL, 4 unreachable. The plan's spawn and POIs sat on cell boundaries (inside walls and pillars).
Moved them to cell centres (-112 + 16i). QA PASS: 873 parts, 216 lights, 4/4 POIs.

## QA rounds 2-3: the lighting was a screenshot problem
- Round 2 shots came out dark and olive (nearly black carpet, grey ceiling). Raising the ambient and the
  troffer lights helped near spawn, but the pillar room stayed dark and the dark wing looked no darker than
  the lit rooms.
- **Cause: `screen_capture` with a camera position grabs the frame before Roblox lights that spot.** Local
  lights (the 216 SurfaceLights) only show where the real Studio camera is. Parking the camera with
  `execute_luau` first, then capturing with no camera arguments, showed the true look: the first tune was
  blown out (white carpet). The kit now has `K.shot(id, name)` for this two-step.
- **The texture parts were still default grey.** Roblox multiplies a MaterialVariant's ColorMap by
  `Part.Color`, so grey darkened every texture by a third. `Materials.apply` now sets white or a tint.
- Final values, all judged on parked-camera shots:
  - `liminal` preset: Ambient 135,127,95; ExposureCompensation 0.45.
  - Lights (map.json): brightness 2, range 20, angle 150 (was 120: hot spots on the walls).
  - Tints (map.json `colors`): floor #A8946A (warmer carpet), ceiling #E8E4DA (lighter tiles), wall #B8B2A6.
- The dark wing now reads dark in the middle with light spilling in from the lit cells round it; the anomaly
  (an outlet at head height) is small but visible from its shot.
- Final: QA PASS, 873 parts, 216 lights (0 with shadows), 4/4 POIs (pillar room 255 studs, dark wing 88,
  hallway end 215, far corner 344, no jumps).

## Sound (Play mode)
The Hum Sound loads, plays and loops: 8.00 s long, volume 0.35, ambient reverb Hallway. The loop seam is
tested in `tests/test_audio.py`; nobody has listened to it yet (do a 10-second listen before filming).
Flicker: 12 material flips in 10 s across the 6 flicker panels. Only the first panel of each cell has a light,
so half the flickers blink the panel without dimming the floor.
